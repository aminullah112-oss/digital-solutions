Be clear on scope

What to send, and what's not included.

Send these with your enquiry

  • Panel type and the customer's specification or single-line sketch
  • System voltage and frequency (e.g. 415 V, 50 Hz), number of poles
  • Incomer and feeder ratings, or connected loads in kW with power factor
  • Prospective fault current at the incomer (kA)
  • Control supply (e.g. 24 V DC or 230 V AC), enclosure IP rating, indoor or outdoor
  • Preferred brands (e.g. L&T, Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Socomec; DSE or ComAp controllers)
  • Any enclosure size, cable-entry or space constraints

Not included (your engineer's call)

  • Cable and busbar sizing
  • Short-circuit withstand verification and temperature-rise assessment
  • Protection coordination and discrimination studies
  • Approval for manufacture: every package is preliminary and must be approved by the client's qualified engineer before building
Questions

Before you send a job.

How long does it take?

It depends on the panel type and how complete the specification is. ATS panels are the quickest because they're generated from one design model. You get a firm delivery date with the quote, before you commit.

What's the difference between "generated" and "engineered to spec"?

ATS panels are produced from a single design model, so the drawings, BOM, layout and 3D model are created together and can't disagree. Other panel types are drawn to your specification on the same ARR Digital Solutions template, with the same checks done by hand.

Which brands can you use?

Any brand with published datasheets. Parts are taken from manufacturer data, with the datasheet recorded against each one. If you have a preferred range, tell me with the enquiry.

Will the prices match my supplier?

Estimates use the distributor price list current at the time, and each priced line records its source and date. If you have your own supplier prices, send them and the BOM will use those.

Can my team edit the drawings?

The layout comes as DXF and the 3D model as STEP, both of which open in standard CAD tools. Drawings are PDF and SVG. The BOM and schedules are Excel.

Is my customer's information kept private?

Yes. Your specifications and drawings are used only for your job, stored on an encrypted machine, and never shared or shown as samples without your written permission.
