ABOUT KAVEEN'S

A local construction partner built around practical needs.

Kaveen's Construction & Abirami Sree Hardwares serves customers looking for project support and related construction materials in Thiruporur and nearby areas. The focus is simple: make sourcing, planning and getting work started easier.

From 2D/3D planning, elevation, interior, structural, electrical and plumbing drawing support, estimation and construction coordination, to everyday building materials, the business brings several project needs into one place.
