Account & Data Deletion

Account & Data Deletion

for the Medwyn Labs mobile application, published by DR SHEELA FEVER LABS AND DIAGNOSTICS OPC PRIVATE LIMITED

How to request deletion

To delete your Medwyn Labs account and associated personal data, email us from the mobile number or email address registered on your account:

Send your request to
drsheelafeverlab@gmail.com
Subject line
Account deletion request

Include your registered mobile number so we can verify your identity before processing the request. We will confirm receipt and complete deletion within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, date of birth, sex, email)
  • Saved addresses
  • Family member profiles linked to your account
  • Push notification tokens
  • In-app notification history

What's retained, and for how long

  • Booking and diagnostic test records (which tests were booked, sample collection and report status) are retained for 7 years after your last booking, as required under Indian medical records retention norms for diagnostic laboratories.
  • Payment transaction records (transaction ID, amount, date) are retained for 8 years, as required under Indian tax and accounting law (Income Tax Act record-keeping requirements).
  • These retained records are kept solely for legal and regulatory compliance and are not used for any other purpose after your account is deleted.

Partial deletion

You can also request deletion of specific data (for example, a single family member's profile, or your saved addresses) without deleting your entire account — specify what you'd like removed in your email.
