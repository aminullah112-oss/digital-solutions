About Oscar Leather Products

A small Melvisharam workshop that does one thing well.

Melvisharam, in Vellore district, is one of Tamil Nadu's leather centres. Oscar Leather Products works from Kattakadai Street making leather keychains — plain, embossed with a brand name, or finished in coloured suede.

If you run a business and need branded keychains in quantity, you deal with the maker directly: send the design, approve a sample, then place the order.
