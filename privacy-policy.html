for the Medwyn Labs mobile application

1 Who this policy covers

Medwyn Labs ("the app") is a diagnostic test booking platform that connects patients with diagnostic laboratories for home sample collection and in-lab testing. It is published by DR SHEELA FEVER LABS AND DIAGNOSTICS OPC PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

The app currently operates in India and is built for Indian phone numbers and Indian payment methods.

2 Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to book and fulfil a diagnostic test. The table below lists each category, why it's collected, and where it's used.

CategoryWhatWhy
Identity & contact Mobile number, name, date of birth, sex, email (optional) Account sign-in (via SMS OTP), identifying who a booking is for, contacting you about it
Location & address Home collection address; precise device location when you use live phlebotomist tracking Routing sample collection to the right address; showing you how far away your phlebotomist is
Health-related Which tests/panels you book, sample status, diagnostic reports, medical history notes you add Core purpose of the app — booking, fulfilling, and delivering diagnostic test results
Payment Transaction ID, amount, payment status Confirming and recording payment for a booking
Voice input (optional) Audio captured only when you tap the microphone icon to dictate text into a form field Converting speech to text using your device's speech recognition
Device & notifications A push-notification token tied to your device Sending booking status updates (e.g. "phlebotomist on the way", "report ready")

We do not collect your precise location in the background — only while you have an active booking screen open that uses it, and only with your permission.

3 Payment information

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a licensed Indian payment gateway. Card, UPI, and net-banking details are entered directly into Razorpay's own secure checkout screen — they pass through Razorpay, never through our servers or this app's own storage. We only ever receive and store a transaction reference, the amount, and whether the payment succeeded.

4 Who we share information with

  • The diagnostic laboratory fulfilling your booking — your name, contact details, address, and the tests ordered, so they can collect your sample and deliver your results.
  • The phlebotomist assigned to your booking — your name, address, and contact number, for sample collection.
  • Razorpay — for payment processing, as described above.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — our backend infrastructure provider. Firebase stores app data, authenticates sign-in via SMS, and delivers push notifications. Google's service terms and privacy practices apply to their handling of this data on our behalf.
  • Google's speech recognition service — only the audio you record when using the optional microphone/dictation feature, processed to return text to the app.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising, and the app does not contain any advertising or analytics-for-marketing SDKs.

5 How long we keep your information

We retain your account and booking data for as long as your account remains active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data as described in Section 7, except where we're required to retain certain records by law (for example, financial transaction records under Indian tax and accounting requirements).

6 Security

  • All data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to your data is restricted by role: a patient can only see their own bookings and family members' records, a lab can only see bookings assigned to it, and a phlebotomist can only see bookings assigned to them.
  • Payment confirmation is verified server-side against Razorpay's own cryptographic signature before any booking is marked paid — the app itself can never mark a payment successful on its own.

7 Deleting your account or data

To request deletion of your account and personal data, email us at the address in Section 10 from the mobile number or email associated with your account. We will confirm your identity and process the request within a reasonable time, except for records we're legally required to keep (see Section 5).

8 Children's data

The app is not directed at children. Where a parent or guardian books a diagnostic test for a child as a family member on their own account, that data is covered by this policy and controlled by the parent/guardian's account, not the child's.

9 Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
